General

What Is the Mission of Holistic Foster Care Foundation?+

We give orphaned and underprivileged girls in Lahore a safe home, and look after their schooling, health, emotional wellbeing and skills, so that one day they can stand on their own.

How Can I Get Involved?+

You can give, sponsor a girl, volunteer or help us spread the word. To talk to someone, call us on +92 322 071 1929.

Who Founded the Foundation?+

Batool Bibi, a nurse with more than twenty years of experience in the United Kingdom, left her career abroad to build a home for girls in her own country.

Where Are You Based?+

Our home is in Model Town, Lahore. We hope that one day this model can be repeated in other parts of Pakistan.

What Makes You Different from Other Orphanages?+

We look after the whole child. Our care rests on four pillars: her mental and emotional wellbeing, her physical health, her education and skills, and her place in the wider community. Each girl gets support shaped around her.

Section 3 · Giving and Donations

Giving and Donations

How Can I Donate?+

You can give by bank transfer. The details are in the Give section of our home page. To set up a regular gift, or to ask about other ways to give, please call us. Online card payment is coming soon.

Can I Give My Zakat?+

Yes. Choose Zakat on the giving form, or tell us when you make your transfer. We will record it as Zakat and use it only for girls who are eligible to receive it.

Where Does My Donation Go?+

Your gift pays for running the home, school fees and materials, healthcare and nutrition, staff salaries and activities that enrich the girls' lives. Financial details are available on request.

Can I Sponsor a Specific Girl?+

Yes. Sponsors support a girl's education, health and development, and receive updates on her progress. Please contact us and we will tell you how it works.

Are Donations Tax-Deductible?+

Donations are tax-deductible in Pakistan because we are a registered non-profit, and we provide an official receipt. If you give from another country, please check your own country's rules.

Section 4 · Volunteering

Volunteering

How Can I Volunteer?+

Get in touch with your skills, your availability and what you would like to do. Everyone who volunteers goes through a screening process first.

Which Volunteer Roles Are Open?+

Roles include tutoring, mentoring, healthcare, IT and computer training, arts and crafts, administration, and fundraising and events.

Can I Volunteer from Abroad or Online?+

Yes. You can tutor or mentor online, help with social media, grant writing or fundraising, or offer professional skills such as web design, graphic design or marketing.

Section 5 · Our Programmes

Our Programmes

What Education Do the Girls Receive?+

Every girl goes to school through our partner schools. At home she gets after-school tutoring, homework help, computer lessons, arts and crafts and life skills, all shaped around her.

How Do You Support Their Emotional Wellbeing?+

Our in-house child psychologist counsels the girls, mentors give them role models to look up to, and group activities build their confidence and resilience.

What Happens When the Girls Grow Up?+

We help with higher education and vocational guidance, finding work, learning to live independently and staying in touch with the girls who came before. Where it is needed, we offer transitional housing.
