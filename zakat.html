Section 2 · What is Zakat

What Is Zakat and Who Can Receive It?

Zakat is the third pillar of Islam. It purifies the giver’s wealth and lifts those who have been left without the basics of life: food, shelter, education and care. For an orphaned girl from a poor family, Zakat can be the difference between the street and a safe home.

Who Is Eligible to Receive Zakat?

The Quran names eight categories of people who may receive Zakat (Surah At-Tawbah, 9:60). Zakat may only be given to people in one of these categories.

At Holistic Foster Care Foundation, we receive your Zakat in strict compliance with Shariah and spend it only on orphaned and underprivileged girls who fall among the poor and the needy categories. Your Zakat donation pays for their meals, clothes, school fees, books, and medical care. It helps us open our doors to more girls who have nowhere else to turn.

Section 4 · Key facts

Key Facts About Zakat in Islam

These are the key facts and rules every Muslim should know before paying Zakat:

Qualification and Nisab of Zakat al-Maal

The Nisab is the minimum amount of wealth that makes Zakat due. It equals the market value of 612.36 grams (52.5 tola) of silver or 87.48 grams (7.5 tola) of gold. If your zakatable wealth reaches the Nisab and stays at or above it for one lunar Islamic year, Zakat is due. Many Islamic scholars in Pakistan advise the silver standard, which is lower and so brings more help to people in need.

Calculation of Zakat al-Maal

Zakat is 2.5% of your net zakatable wealth, paid every lunar year to the eight categories named in the Quran. To work out your exact amount in PKR, use our free online Zakat calculator.
Open the Zakat Calculator

Zakat on Gold and Silver

In the Hanafi school, followed by most Muslims in Pakistan, gold and silver are zakatable even when worn as jewellery or adornment. Always include your gold and silver jewellery, at today’s value, when you calculate your Zakat.

Section 5 · Dignity

Pay Zakat Online and Protect Her Dignity

Islam teaches us to give quietly, so that the person who receives never feels lessened. When you pay Zakat online, you give in private, and the girls never know whose Zakat paid for their school shoes or their medicine. They simply know they are cared for.

“If you disclose your charity, it is good; but if you conceal it and give it to the poor, it is better for you.” Surah Al-Baqarah, 2:271

Zakat al-Fitr (Fitrana)

Besides Zakat al-Maal, Zakat al-Fitr, also called Fitrana, is due from every Muslim who has the means, for themselves and each person they support. It purifies the fast and helps those in need celebrate Eid. Pay it before the Eid prayer. At HFCF, your Fitrana gives the girls food and Eid clothes, so no girl wakes on Eid morning feeling forgotten.

Zakat in Ramadan

Many Muslims give their Zakat in Ramadan, when every good deed is multiplied, and hope for the reward of Laylatul Qadr. If your Zakat date falls outside Ramadan, you may pay your Zakat in advance during the blessed month. Never delay Zakat that is already due. Plan your Laylatul Qadr Zakat and give on every one of the last ten nights.

Section 7 · Our eligibility process

Our Zakat Eligibility Process

Holistic Foster Care Foundation holds a Shariah-compliance certificate, and a qualified Islamic scholar oversees how we receive and spend Zakat. Before any girl benefits from Zakat, we follow these steps:

1

We Record Her Background

When a girl is referred to us, we record her details: her family situation, whether she has lost one or both parents, her guardian’s details, address, schooling and health needs.

2

We Assess Her Family’s Means

We record what her family or guardian owns and owes, including cash, gold and silver, savings, property and debts, to establish whether she is among the poor and the needy. Losing a parent alone does not make a child eligible for Zakat. Her family must also lack the means to provide for her.

3

We Verify the Information

Our team checks the key details, including documents and a home visit where possible, before she is accepted for Zakat support.

4

Her Guardian Gives Consent

Her guardian signs a form confirming the family’s circumstances and authorising Holistic Foster Care Foundation to receive and spend Zakat on her behalf.

5

Our Shariah Adviser Decides

The final decision on Zakat eligibility rests with our Shariah adviser, whose approval is recorded on her assessment form. We do not use Zakat for any girl without that approval.

Ownership passes to the girl. Ownership of what Zakat buys, such as food, clothing, books and medicine, passes to the girl herself (tamleek), as Shariah requires.

Section 8 · FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

What Is Zakat in Islam?+

Zakat is the third pillar of Islam. It is an obligatory charity on every adult, sane Muslim whose wealth reaches the Nisab. It purifies your wealth spiritually and provides for the poor and the needy, including orphaned children who have no one to support them.

Is It Permissible to Pay Zakat Online?+

Yes. Scholars agree that paying Zakat online is permissible, safe and convenient. Make sure you give to a Shariah-compliant organisation that keeps Zakat separate and spends it only on eligible people, as Holistic Foster Care Foundation does.

What Does Zakat Mean?+

Zakat comes from the Arabic word zakah, meaning purification and growth. Zakat purifies lawful (halal) wealth and blesses what remains. It cannot purify wealth earned by unlawful (haram) means.

How Is Zakat Calculated?+

Add up the value of all your zakatable wealth, subtract the debts and bills due now, and give 2.5% of the remainder, if it is at or above the Nisab. Items for personal use, such as your home, car, furniture and clothes, are not included. Pay your Zakat on time each lunar year.

How Much Zakat Do I Need to Pay?+

You pay 2.5% of your net zakatable wealth, as long as it is equal to or more than the Nisab. The amount depends on what you own on your Zakat date.

Why Is Zakat Important?+

Zakat is one of the five pillars of Islam and a duty Allah (SWT) placed on those who can afford it. It is an act of worship that purifies your wealth, and it sustains people who would otherwise go without, like an orphaned girl who needs a home, a school and someone to care for her.

Who Does Not Have to Pay Zakat?+

People whose wealth is below the Nisab do not pay Zakat. In the Hanafi school, children and people with mental disabilities are also exempt.

Can I Pay Zakat in Ramadan If My Zakat Date Falls Outside It?+

You should not delay Zakat once it is due. But if your Zakat date falls later in the year, you may pay it in advance during Ramadan and receive the blessings of the sacred month.

Can Zakat Be Used to Care for Orphans?+

Yes, when the orphan is among the poor or the needy (Fuqara or Masakin). Being an orphan alone does not make a child eligible. A child who has inherited wealth, or whose family can provide for her, should not receive Zakat. That is why we assess every girl’s eligibility, spend Zakat only on what belongs to her, such as her food, clothing, schooling and healthcare, and make sure ownership passes to her.

Will the Girls Know That My Donation Is Zakat?+

No. Your intention when you give is what counts, and the girls are never told. Their dignity is always protected.
