“Indeed, We sent it down on the Night of Decree. And what will make you know what the Night of Decree is? The Night of Decree is better than a thousand months. The angels and the Spirit descend in it by permission of their Lord with every matter. Peace it is, until the rising of the dawn.”

Section 4 · Significance

Significance of Laylatul Qadr

Surah Al-Qadr is dedicated entirely to Laylatul Qadr, and Surah Ad-Dukhan also describes it as “a blessed night” (44:3).

The Quran was revealed on this night. It is also the Night of Decree. Islamic scholars explain that on it, the affairs of the coming year, such as life, death, and provision, are set out and entrusted to the angel.

Section 5 · Benefits

Laylatul Qadr Benefits

The Quran makes the reward of this night clear. Any act of worship done on Laylatul Qadr is better than the same act done for a thousand months. A thousand months is about 83 years and 4 months, longer than most of us will live.

That is why believers offer Nawafil, recite the Quran, make dua and give charity on every one of the last ten nights of Ramadan. The Prophet (peace be upon him) told us why this night must not be missed:

The Prophet (peace be upon him) said, “Whoever stands in prayer on the Night of Qadr out of sincere faith and hoping for reward from Allah, all his previous sins will be forgiven; and whoever fasts the month of Ramadan out of sincere faith and hoping for reward from Allah, all his previous sins will be forgiven.” Sahih al-Bukhari 1901

Section 7 · When is it

When Is Laylatul Qadr 2027?

The exact date of Laylatul Qadr has not been revealed, but authentic hadith place it on one of the odd nights of the last ten nights of Ramadan. Many Muslims give special attention to the 27th night, but scholars advise seeking it across the whole of the last Ashara.

Section 8 · Why it is hidden

Why the Date of Laylatul Qadr Is Hidden

The Quran and Sunnah tell us how great this night is, yet its exact date was kept from us. The Prophet (peace be upon him) explained why:

“The Prophet (peace be upon him) came out to inform us about the Night of Qadr, but two Muslims were quarrelling with each other. So he said, ‘I came out to inform you about the Night of Qadr, but such-and-such persons were quarrelling, so the knowledge of it was taken away; yet that might be for your own good, so search for it on the 29th, 27th and 25th.’” Sahih al-Bukhari 2023

Because no one knows the night, the surest way to give on Laylatul Qadr is to give on all ten nights, especially the odd-numbered ones.

Section 9 · What to do

What Should We Do on Laylatul Qadr?

As you seek Laylatul Qadr in the last Ashara of Ramadan, these acts of devotion bring great reward:

  • Offering Nawafil and standing in Qiyam and Tahajjud
  • Reciting the Quran, the Book sent down on this very night
  • Dhikr: remembering Allah (SWT) and making sincere dua
  • Giving charity, especially to the orphan and the needy
  • Showing kindness: to your family, your neighbours, and the people who need you most

Increase Your Chances of Catching Laylatul Qadr

  • Give charity every night of the last Ashara, so your gift is certain to fall on the Night of Power.
  • Give more on the odd nights: the 21st, 23rd, 25th, 27th and 29th.
  • Give generously on the 27th night, when many hope Laylatul Qadr falls.

Section 11 · FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

What Is Laylatul Qadr?+

Laylatul Qadr is known as the Night of Power or the Night of Decree. The Quran declares it better than a thousand months, so any charity or good deed done on this night brings immense reward and blessings.

How Can We Secure the Rewards of the Night of Power?+

By praying, reciting the Quran, making sincere dua and giving charity on every one of the last ten nights of Ramadan.

Is Laylatul Qadr Mentioned in the Quran?+

Yes. “Indeed, We sent it down on the Night of Decree” (Surah Al-Qadr, 97:1). The whole of Surah Al-Qadr is about this night.

Is Laylatul Qadr Mentioned in Hadith?+

Yes. Several authentic hadith say it falls in the last ten nights of Ramadan, especially the odd nights (Sahih al-Bukhari 2017).

When Should We Search for Laylatul Qadr?+

Across the last ten nights (the last Ashara) of Ramadan, and especially on the odd nights.

What Is the Significance of Worship on This Night?+

Worship on Laylatul Qadr is better than a thousand months of worship. It includes reciting the Quran, praying Nawafil, making dua and giving Sadaqah.

What Are the Signs of the Night of Power?+

The Quran describes it as a night of peace until dawn (97:5). The Prophet (peace be upon him) said that on the morning after it, the sun rises without strong rays (Sahih Muslim 762).

Can I Give My Zakat on Laylatul Qadr?+

Yes, if it is due then or you are paying it in advance. Never delay Zakat that is already due to wait for Laylatul Qadr. Mark your gift “Zakat” so it goes to our separate Zakat fund and is spent only on eligible girls.

Where Does My Laylatul Qadr Gift Go at HFCF?+

It cares for orphaned and underprivileged girls in our Pakistan home. A safe place to live, three healthy meals a day, school and tutoring, healthcare and counselling, and the love of a family.
