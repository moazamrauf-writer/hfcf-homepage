Section 5 · Girls' stories (scrolling)

Meet the Girls

With Your Help, a Future Teacher

Areeba

There was a time when Areeba hardly spoke. Today she's one of the chattiest members of her family, full of thoughts to share and questions to ask. The way she explains things to her friends, you can easily picture her as a teacher.

"I want Areeba to go to school, to learn and to become a teacher." Her father
With Your Help, a Future Doctor

Seerat

Seerat's eyes say a lot about her. They are curious, expressive and full of warmth. She loves reading, debating and exploring the world through computer games. After losing her father she has slowly built her confidence, and at HFCF she is beginning to believe in what she can do.

"Seerat is a sensitive girl with a big heart. I want her to study and become a doctor so that she can help the sick." Her mother
With Your Help, a Future Psychologist

Ayesha

Ayesha has a beautiful smile and a strong hunger to learn. She loves reading, acting and badminton, and recites the Holy Quran in a lovely, clear voice. She has carried a great deal for her age, and it has given her a dream: to support and guide others through their struggles.

"My daughter has a gentle heart. She wants to help people who are hurting." Her mother
With Your Help, a Future Artist

Noor ul Ain

Give Noor a pencil and a sheet of paper, and her imagination appears on the page. She is calm, thoughtful and observant, with a rare talent for drawing and sketching. At HFCF, her creativity and her confidence are being nurtured, so that her gift can become the start of her future.

"She draws whenever she can. I want her to study well and to become an artist one day." Her mother
With Your Help, a Future Army Officer

Arfa

Arfa has the kind of energy that fills a room. She loves football, throws herself into everything she tries, and says “let me try” before anyone has finished explaining. She is building consistency in her studies, and she dreams of serving her country with strength, discipline and pride.

"My daughter has always been strong. I only want her to study, to stay safe and to grow into someone who can stand tall." Her mother
With Your Help, a Future Army Nurse

Misbah

Misbah has a smile and a pair of dimples that melt hearts. Gentle and patient, she is always the first to share what she has with others. She is showing great promise in her studies and on stage, and she dreams of serving others with courage and dedication.

"Misbah is a kind and gentle child. I want her to study and to become a nurse who helps people." Her father

Section 8 · About us (It began with care)

It Began with Care

Batool Bibi spent more than twenty years nursing in the United Kingdom, including in flood- and earthquake-hit communities. For years she set aside part of her earnings, dreaming of doing something worthwhile for children in Pakistan.

So she came home and built a place that feels like a home, not an institution. Today, in Lahore, our girls live, learn and grow up together, with people who know each of them by name.

"I cannot change the circumstances into which every child is born. But I can do my best to make sure those circumstances do not determine her future." Batool Bibi · Founder & Chairperson
