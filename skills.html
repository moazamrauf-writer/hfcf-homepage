Every girl is good at something. We give her the time, the tools and the encouragement to find out what.

Section 2 · What she gets

What She Gets

Art and Crafts

Hands-on creative work that lets her say what she cannot yet put into words.

Computers and IT

Computer training that opens doors to work and study later on.

Speaking Up

Debate and communication practice, so she learns that her voice matters.

Life Skills

Practical skills for everyday life, including how to handle money.

Section 3 · Beyond the classroom

Beyond the Classroom

Cultural Celebrations

Festivals and special days celebrated together, so she feels she belongs to something bigger.

Service and Outings

Community service projects and field trips that show her the world beyond the gate.

Quran and Hifz

Time set aside for the Quran, for the girls who want to learn it by heart.
