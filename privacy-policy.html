Section 2 · The details Last updated: 21 November 2026
To confirm: this is a plain-language draft written for the foundation. Please have the foundation’s legal adviser review it before launch.

Who We Are

Holistic Foster Care Foundation is a home for orphaned girls in Lahore, Pakistan. We are a Section 42 company registered with the Securities and Exchange Commission of Pakistan (Licence No. 3066). This policy explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it, and how we look after it. You can reach us at info@holisticfostercare.org or on +92 322 071 1929.

What We Collect

We collect only what we need.

  • When you give: your name, phone number, email address and postal address (so we can send your receipt), the amount, and the kind of gift (Zakat, Sadaqah or a general donation). For bank transfers, we also see the details on your transfer and the receipt you send us on WhatsApp.
  • When you write to us or sign up: your name, email address, phone number and your message.
  • When you visit our website: the basic technical information your browser sends, such as your IP address, device and the pages you open. Our hosting provider keeps this in ordinary server logs. We do not run advertising trackers. If we add website analytics, we will update this policy first.

The Zakat Calculator

The figures you type into our Zakat Calculator stay on your device. We do not receive them and we do not store them. To fill in today’s gold and silver prices, the calculator asks two outside services for current rates, and they may see your IP address in the normal way, as any website you visit would.

How We Use Your Information

  • To process your gift and send your official receipt.
  • To record your Zakat separately from other gifts, as Shariah requires.
  • To keep our accounts and meet the legal and audit duties of a registered charity.
  • To tell you how your gift is helping, and to share news and appeals, but only if you have asked us to.
  • To answer your questions and keep the girls in our care safe.

Online Payments

Online card payments are being set up. When they are live, your card details will be handled directly by a secure, licensed payment provider. We will never see or store your full card number. Until then, you can give by bank transfer using the details on our giving pages.

Who We Share It With

We never sell or rent your personal information. We share it only with:

  • payment providers and banks, so your gift can reach us;
  • accountants and auditors, who must keep it confidential;
  • companies that help us run our website and email, under strict confidentiality;
  • government bodies and regulators, where the law requires it.

We do not publish donors’ names without their permission.

Our Girls’ Privacy

We take great care with the images and stories of the girls. We share them only with proper consent and safeguarding checks. Please do not copy, edit or reshare them without our written permission.

Keeping It Safe, and for How Long

We use sensible technical and organisational measures to protect your information. We keep donation records for as long as the law and our auditors require. We keep your email address on our mailing list until you ask us to remove it.

Your Choices

You can ask us to show you what we hold about you, correct it, or delete it, except where the law requires us to keep it. You can unsubscribe from our emails at any time using the link in them, or by writing to us. We will reply as soon as we can.

Cookies

Our website uses only what it needs to work. We do not use advertising cookies.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of the page shows when it last changed.
