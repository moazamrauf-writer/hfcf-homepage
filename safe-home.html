How We Care

A Safe Home.

Before anything else, a child needs to feel safe. Our home is built around warm routines and people who show up every day.

Section 2 · What she gets

What She Gets

A Bed of Her Own

A safe place to sleep, with a caretaker nearby, every night of the year.

People Who Show Up

A warden and caretakers who know each girl by name and are there every day.

Someone Who Knows Her

A care plan shaped around each girl, not one routine for everyone.

Part of Her Community

Outings, celebrations and service projects, so she grows up as part of the world, not apart from it.

Section 3 · Keeping her safe

Looking After Her Safety

Safety starts with people. Our caretakers and warden are with the girls every day, our in-house psychologist is there when something feels wrong, and everyone who volunteers with us goes through a screening process first.

Each girl has a care plan shaped around her, so the adults around her know what she needs, not just what the rules say.

Needs input · Safeguarding policy

The Foundation's safeguarding and child-protection policy will be summarised here once we have it. Until then, this page says only what we can stand behind.

Section 4 · When she grows up

When She Grows Up

A home should not end on a girl's eighteenth birthday. We offer guidance on higher education and vocational training, help finding work, lessons in living independently, support from the girls who came before her and, where it is needed, a place to stay while she finds her feet.
