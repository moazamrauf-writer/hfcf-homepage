A Home for Girls, Built on Care.

Section 2 · Our story

Our Story

Batool Bibi spent more than twenty years nursing in the United Kingdom, including in flood- and earthquake-hit communities. For years she set aside part of her earnings, dreaming of doing something worthwhile for children in Pakistan.

What stayed with her was the struggle of girls back home. In the end she left her nursing career, came to Lahore and built a place that feels like a home, not an institution.

Today our girls live, learn and grow up together, with people who know each of them by name.

"I cannot change the circumstances into which every child is born. But I can do my best to make sure those circumstances do not determine her future." Batool Bibi · Founder & Chairperson
Section 3 · What we believe

What We Believe

Our Mission

To empower underprivileged girls through education, care and a supportive community.

Our Vision

A world where every girl can thrive and build a better future.

Our Values

Compassion, education, empowerment and community.

We care for the whole girl: her health, her feelings, her schooling and her place among others, so that one day she can build an independent life of her own.
