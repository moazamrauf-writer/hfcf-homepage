Section 2 · Ways to partner

Ways to Partner with Us

Corporate Giving

Direct your company’s Zakat and CSR giving to girls’ food, schooling and healthcare, with clear records and official receipts.

Ramadan and Eid Campaigns

Run a Ramadan or Eid drive with your staff and customers. We will plan it with you and share how it helped.

Gifts in Kind

Give school bags, books, warm clothing, bedding or medical supplies that go straight to the girls who need them.

Skills and Professional Support

Teach, mentor, treat, counsel, or help with law, accounts and design. Your skills can change a girl’s future.

Schools and Communities

Bring your school, mosque or neighbourhood together for a fundraiser or a visit with our girls.

Media and Awareness

Help tell the story of orphaned girls in Pakistan, with care for the girls’ privacy and dignity.

Section 3 · What to expect

What You Can Expect from Us

Transparency

An official receipt for every gift, and clear records of how it is used.

Shariah Compliance

Zakat is kept in a separate fund and spent only on eligible girls.

Child Safeguarding

The girls’ safety comes first. We agree how images and stories are used before anything is shared.

Honest Reporting

We tell you how your support helped, in plain words.

Section 4 · How to start

How to Start

1

Reach Out

Write to us or message us on WhatsApp and tell us how you would like to help.

2

We Talk It Through

We listen, share what the girls need most, and suggest a way that suits you.

3

We Plan It Together

We agree what you will give, how it will be used, and how we will report back.
