Section 2 · Rewards

Sadaqah and Its Immense Rewards

Sadaqah is given to draw closer to Allah (SWT). It isn’t only money. A kind word, a smile or an hour of your time is Sadaqah too. The Prophet (peace be upon him) said, “Every act of kindness is Sadaqah” (Sahih al-Bukhari 6021). In many ways, Sadaqah is the Islamic way of life put into practice.

Sadaqah also protects the one who gives it.

“Protect yourselves from the Fire, even with half a date.” Sahih al-Bukhari 1417

Through Holistic Foster Care Foundation, your Sadaqah reaches girls who have lost a parent, a home or both, and gives them a family again.

Section 7 · FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

What Is Sadaqah in Islam?+

Sadaqah is voluntary charity. It is given out of compassion, purely for the sake of Allah (SWT), with no wish for anything in return.

What Does Sadaqah Mean?+

The word comes from the Arabic root sidq, meaning truthfulness and sincerity. Giving Sadaqah shows the truth of your faith.

Can Sadaqah Be Used to Care for Orphans?+

Yes. Sadaqah can pay for every part of an orphaned girl’s care: her food, clothes, schooling and healthcare, and also the home, carers and teachers that keep her safe.

How Is Sadaqah Different from Zakat?+

Zakat is obligatory: 2.5% of your wealth above the Nisab, paid each lunar year to specific recipients. Sadaqah is voluntary, and you can give it at any time, in any amount. At HFCF, Zakat pays only for eligible girls’ direct needs, while Sadaqah can also pay for the home and the people who run it.

What Is the Spiritual Importance of Sadaqah?+

Sadaqah is given sincerely to seek Allah’s (SWT) pleasure. It eases hardship for others, softens and purifies the heart, and brings reward in this life and the next.

What Is Sadaqah Jariyah?+

Sadaqah Jariyah is ongoing charity: a gift that keeps benefiting people over time, and keeps bringing reward to the giver, even after death.

What Are Examples of Sadaqah Jariyah at HFCF?+

Paying for a girl’s education, Quran lessons or computer skills; furnishing a classroom, the computer lab or the girls’ bedrooms; and any lasting project that will serve girl after girl for years.

Is Sadaqah Only About Money?+

No. Kindness, a helping hand, sharing what you know and giving your time are all Sadaqah. You can also volunteer with HFCF as a tutor, mentor or in a professional skill. To confirm: volunteer roles

How Does Sadaqah Bring Blessings?+

Sadaqah eases someone’s hardship, shields the giver, wipes away sins and brings barakah into what remains. Allah (SWT) promises to replace whatever you spend in His way.

Can I Give Sadaqah on Behalf of Someone Who Has Died?+

Yes. According to authentic hadith, the reward of Sadaqah given for a deceased parent or relative reaches them. Add their name to your donation note.

How Do I Give Sadaqah Online with HFCF?+

Choose an amount, select one-time or monthly giving, and complete your donation through your preferred payment method. After a bank transfer, WhatsApp your receipt with the word “Sadaqah” to +92 322 0711929 for your official receipt.

Does Furnishing a Classroom or Donating Learning Equipment Count as Sadaqah Jariyah?+

Yes. If it serves many girls and lasts a long time, such as computers, books, desks or beds, it can be a lasting source of Sadaqah Jariyah.
