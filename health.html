How We Care

Good Health, Body and Mind.

A child who is hungry, unwell or frightened cannot learn. We look after her body and her feelings together.

Section 1 · Intro
Section 2 · What she gets

What She Gets

Balanced Meals

Nutritious meals, planned with a nutritionist, so she grows up strong.

Regular Check-Ups

Medical check-ups on a regular basis, with dental and vision care too.

Someone to Talk To

Counselling with our in-house child psychologist, and mentors who give her someone to look up to.

Active and Well

Physical activities that keep her fit, and group sessions that build confidence and resilience.
