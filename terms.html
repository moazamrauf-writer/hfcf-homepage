About These Terms

These terms apply when you use the Holistic Foster Care Foundation website or give to us. By using the site or giving, you agree to them. Please also read our Privacy Policy.

Your Gifts

Every gift is voluntary. If you made a gift by mistake, or were charged twice, please contact us as soon as you notice and we will look into it. Otherwise, gifts are not refundable, because they are put to work for the girls straight away.

How We Use Your Gift

  • Zakat: we record Zakat in a separate fund. It is spent only on girls who are eligible to receive it, and whom our Shariah adviser has approved. It is not used for salaries, buildings or running costs.
  • Sadaqah and general donations: these go where they are needed most for the girls’ care, including food, schooling, healthcare and the home itself.
  • Named appeals: if a gift is given to a named appeal and the appeal receives more than it needs, we will use the surplus for a similar purpose that helps the girls.

Receipts and Tax

We send an official receipt for every gift. Tax treatment depends on your own circumstances, so please ask your tax adviser how it applies to you.

Monthly Giving

When online giving is available, you can stop a monthly gift at any time by writing to us or contacting your payment provider.

The Zakat Calculator and Information

Our Zakat Calculator, and the information on this site, are a guide only. They are not a religious ruling or financial advice. The Nisab we use is the figure notified by the Government of Pakistan for the stated Zakat year. Gold, silver and exchange rates come from outside services and may differ from your local market. For complex cases, please ask a qualified scholar.
